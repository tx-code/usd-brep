Native STEP import offers an explicit, source-hash-bound repair of two conflicting
outer-bound markers on a proven finite oriented cylindrical band. It retains the
original source rejection, proves the region with bounded exact arithmetic,
verifies unchanged native three-dimensional definitions and tolerances, and
publishes the complete selected request as AcceptedWithRepairs. The standalone
viewer transports the plan and distinguishes boundary-role repair in its HUD.

## Scope

[Exact cylindrical-band repair contract](../source/STEP_IMPORT/README.md).

## Integrator action

Rebuild STEP_SEMANTICS, STEP_IMPORT, native consumers and the viewer worker
together. Provide a complete original-file-bound plan and explicit budgets;
retain the owning source and repair evidence with any native export.

## Compatibility

Internal source checking, Options/Report and snapshot evidence expand. Default
Repair=None, geometry budgets, kernel ABI and native archive format are unchanged.
This pilot cannot be combined with other repair, definition selection or domain/
periodic normalization requests. It neither rewrites source nor licenses a
general healer, sampled region proof, failed-content dropping or source recovery.
