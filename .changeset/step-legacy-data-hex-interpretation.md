An independent explicit carrier policy interprets lowercase hexadecimal digits
in DATA X/X2/X4 strings after declaration 1 has been selected under the corrected
encoding assumption. Immutable per-string spans/counts/completion evidence and
the original strict refusal accompany the source and native owning result.

## Scope

[STEP source inspection](../source/STEP_SOURCE/README.md), semantic/native CLIs
and the independent benchmark probe. Failed payloads and quotas retain evidence
without publishing a partial graph/model. Opaque bytes are not guessed or decoded.

## Integrator action

Rebuild source, semantic, native libraries and consumers for the extended carrier
receipt. Choose Level1AsCorrectedEncodingWithLowercaseHex explicitly; typed/native
use also requires general interoperability. Retain the report and interpretive
qualification; check each string's completion state on failed inspections.

## Compatibility

Defaults, the old carrier policy, HEADER strings and declarations other than 1
retain their prior behavior. This is not source rewriting, arbitrary escape
recovery or authenticated original-1994 semantics. Native success stays separate
as AcceptedUnderInterpretation; conflicting CLI carrier choices are rejected.
