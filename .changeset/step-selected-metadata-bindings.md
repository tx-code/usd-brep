General interoperability can retain opaque selected-part identifiers, the
selected material BRep caption and attached approval/person labels after exact
field/ownership proofs. Distinct PC/PDC application identities and their APDs
follow source references instead of an arbitrary global singleton or text merge.

## Scope

[STEP source semantics](../source/STEP_SEMANTICS/README.md). The required shape,
unit and topology graph remains fully checked within the declared source subset;
representation/PDS purposes, discriminators and unknown owners are not captions.

## Integrator action

Select general geometry, raw-byte capture and nongeometric interoperability
explicitly. Keep OpaqueString text unavailable and retain original diagnostics.
Inspect the separate definition-application/protocol roles; the legacy scalar
APD view describes the product-associated APD and never selects a schema edition.

## Compatibility

Strict/P0 and existing single-context paths stay unchanged. Multiple/conflicting
APDs for one context, unreferenced contexts, extra identity definitions or
physical misuse cannot gain these proofs. No revision/name/encoding is invented;
this does not establish all management or global AP conformance.
