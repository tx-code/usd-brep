Explicit general STEP import recognizes a single bare `CONFIG_CONTROL_DESIGN`
through a separately identified modified-N916 source subset. The report preserves
the name-only HEADER and records that its edition is unverified. Required fields,
administrative SELECT branches and unit prerequisites use the pinned N916 source;
they do not inherit N2635's different optionality or conversion-unit WHERE clause.
Explicit nongeometric interoperability can retain an attached foreign layer and
an absent APD with their original diagnostics, without manufacturing source roles.

## Scope

[Native import](../source/STEP_IMPORT/README.md) and
[source checking](../source/STEP_SEMANTICS/README.md).

## Integrator action

Rebuild STEP libraries and consumers. Handle the appended `Ap203N916Source`
profile kind and inspect the selected scope, preserved diagnostics and native
construction result. General source admission does not guarantee every geometry
representation can be constructed.

## Compatibility

Default/P0 behavior and the four existing catalog identities remain unchanged.
Only explicit general scope selects the new implementation. HEADER identity,
source bytes, units, quality budgets, repair and model ownership are unchanged.
Source/report recognition and the profile-kind enum are extended. Catalog
snapshot v2 covers five profiles; v1 keeps its historical four-profile meaning.
