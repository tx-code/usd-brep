General STEP source inspection retains one explicitly associated bounded wireframe
alongside its selected BRep, preserving separate source roles and checking the
admitted curve-set members and wireframe prerequisites. Native construction has
its own geometry, quality and complete-publication gates.

## Scope

[Native STEP source contract](../source/STEP_SEMANTICS/README.md).

## Integrator action

Select general source scope to inspect this graph. Do not treat a checked source
snapshot as native acceptance or infer composition from a representation relationship.

## Compatibility

General source catalogs append three declarations without moving existing type IDs;
linked graphs expose two additional source roles. P0 and ordinary source behavior
remain unchanged. No C++ API/ABI, quality, native-file or ownership contract changes.
